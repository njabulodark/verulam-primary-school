## VERULAM TOWN INFO (for About section)

Use this context when writing the Verulam section:
- Founded March 13, 1850 by British Methodist settlers (400 pioneers led by Thomas Champean)
- Named after the Earl of Verulam (James Grimston, 3rd Earl)
- Third oldest settlement in KwaZulu-Natal (after Durban and Pietermaritzburg)
- 175th anniversary celebrated in 2025
- Predominantly Indian South African community with Zulu and English residents
- Verulam Market opened 1884 (historic fresh produce market)
- 24km north of Durban CBD
- Part of eThekwini Metropolitan Municipality
- Multicultural: Hindu, Muslim, Christian communities
- Known for Verulam Temple, heritage centre, and Town Hall
