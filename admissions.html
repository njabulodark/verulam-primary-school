Admissions Policy

Following the South African Schools Act of 1996 guidelines.

1

Age Requirements

Children aged 7-15 years must attend school. Grade R entry requires turning 5 by April 30, Grade 1 requires turning 7 by April 30.

2

Registration Period

Applications open from April to October for the following academic year, as per provincial education department guidelines.

3

Documentation

Submit birth certificates, immunization cards, and additional documentation for non-South African citizens.

4

Enrollment

Upon acceptance, complete enrollment with the school office. As a Quintile 4 school, fees and levies apply.

Requirements

Required Documentation

Per the South African Schools Act of 1996, the following documents are required for enrollment.

📋

💉

Complete immunization records as per Department of Health requirements.

🆔

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

When do applications open?

Applications for the following academic year open in April and close in October, as per the South African Schools Act of 1996. Late applications may be considered based on availability.

What are the age requirements?

Grade R: Must turn 5 by April 30 of enrollment year. Grade 1: Must turn 7 by April 30. Children aged 7-15 years must attend school (compulsory education).

Do you charge school fees?

No. As a Quintile 4 fee-paying school, Verulam Primary charges school fees and levies to maintain our high standard of education and facilities.

Do you accept mid-year transfers?

Mid-year admissions are considered based on space availability in the relevant grade level. Please contact our office at 032 533 1347.
